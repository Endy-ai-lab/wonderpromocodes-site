Wonder in Natick

Wonder's first move into MetroWest Boston.

Natick was one of the three kitchens Wonder opened on February 12, 2026, when the company crossed into Massachusetts for the first time. The choice made sense. MetroWest is dense enough to support a 35-minute delivery zone, sits at the intersection of Routes 9 and 27 (so couriers can reach Wayland, Framingham, and Wellesley quickly), and has a customer base that skews suburban-professional rather than urban-late-night.

The kitchen is on Worcester Street near the Natick Mall, which is the practical center of the zone. From there, deliveries reach all of Natick proper, most of Wayland, the eastern half of Framingham, and the western edge of Wellesley. Boston was Wonder's biggest market expansion since Grubhub, and Natick was specifically chosen as the testbed because of the suburban density and the strong restaurant-delivery baseline already in place.

Worth flagging for early users: this is a newer kitchen, so the menu lineup occasionally rotates as Wonder dials in what MetroWest customers actually order. The standard chef partners (Bobby Flay, José Andrés, Michael Symon, Di Fara) are stable. A few of the smaller restaurant features come and go. The ANDY626 discount has worked here since launch and applies to whatever's on the menu the day you order.

Natick questions

Things people in Natick ask before ordering.

When did Wonder come to Natick?
February 12, 2026. Natick was one of the first three Wonder kitchens to open in Massachusetts, alongside Belmont and Framingham. The MetroWest expansion was the largest single market opening Wonder had done since the Grubhub acquisition the year before.
Where is the Natick Wonder kitchen?
1245 Worcester Street, Natick, MA 01760. The location is just off Route 9, near the Natick Mall. Pickup is available at a small counter; there is no dine-in seating at this kitchen.
Does Wonder deliver to Wayland, Framingham, and Wellesley?
Yes, partially. Wayland east of Route 27 is in zone, as is most of east Framingham (especially around the Route 9 corridor and Saxonville). Wellesley is covered along the western edge near Route 9. West Framingham orders typically route to the Framingham kitchen instead, which sits closer to those addresses.
Why isn't Wonder open as late in Natick as in NYC or Hoboken?
Natick closes at 11:00 pm because suburban late-night demand is much smaller than urban. The kitchen opens earlier in the day relative to demand and closes earlier in the night, which is the typical pattern for Wonder's MetroWest sites. Saturdays sometimes run a bit later by an hour during peak demand stretches.
Does ANDY626 work for new Wonder customers in Massachusetts?
Yes. ANDY626 is a Wonder-wide referral code that applies at any kitchen across the Northeast, including all of the Massachusetts locations. New customers get $15 off their first order and another $15 off their second order, $30 total. The Natick kitchen has accepted the code since it opened.
Is the menu the same as in New York?
The chef-partner core is the same: Bobby Flay, José Andrés, Michael Symon, Marcus Samuelsson, Di Fara, and so on. A few of the smaller restaurant features rotate per market, so a specific dish you saw in NYC may or may not be on the Natick menu on a given day. The big-name menus are stable.
Will Wonder add more Boston-area locations?
Yes. Wonder has stated publicly that the Boston metro is one of its priority growth markets through 2026, with several additional kitchens listed as 'coming soon' on its locations directory. Natick was the first; expect Newton, Brookline, Cambridge, and others over the coming months.
