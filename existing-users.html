Existing customers · August 2026

Saving on Wonder when you're already a customer.

The honest answer: most "Wonder promo code for existing customers" claims you'll find on coupon sites are stale or fake. Here's what actually works, with real specifics on each option.

First, the bad news

The new-customer code does not work for existing accounts.

Wonder's main promotional lever is its referral program. New customers get $30 off across their first two orders ($15 each) when someone shares a referral code with them. Code ANDY626 is one such referral code; there are thousands of others tied to thousands of existing customer accounts. They all work the same way and they all share one rule: new accounts only.

If you have an existing Wonder account, the referral code field at checkout will reject any new-customer code. You cannot work around this by signing up with a different email; Wonder ties accounts to phone number and address. Coupon sites that claim a working "$15 off Wonder for existing customers" code are almost always either out of date or were never accurate to begin with.

That said, there are four legitimate ways existing Wonder customers can save. Below is what each one actually does.

Option 1

Wonder+ membership ($7.99/month).

Wonder+ removes the per-order service fee. The fee on a typical Wonder order is around $1.99-$2.99, which means Wonder+ pays for itself if you place more than 3-4 orders per month. Heavy Wonder users (10+ orders/month) save real money on this; light users won't get value out of it.

Wonder+ does not affect menu prices, doesn't unlock new restaurants, and doesn't give you priority delivery. It's strictly a fee-removal subscription. You can sign up for it inside the Wonder app under "Account" and cancel anytime.

Verdict: worth it if you're a regular customer, not worth it for occasional orders.

Option 2

In-app promotions and seasonal deals.

Wonder pushes promotional offers to existing customers through the app's "Offers" section and via push notifications. These are typically restaurant-specific (e.g., "$5 off any Bobby's Burgers order this week") or behavioral ("we miss you, here's $10 off your next order if you haven't ordered in 30 days").

Two practical things to know about these:

Win-back offers ("we miss you") tend to appear after about 3-4 weeks of no orders. If you're an active user who orders regularly, you won't see these. If you've drifted off the platform for a month, log into the app and check the Offers tab; there's a good chance there's something waiting for you.

Seasonal promos appear around major holidays and during slower delivery windows (early afternoon weekdays, mid-morning weekends). The app's notification settings include a toggle for promotional alerts; turn it on to catch these.

Verdict: not predictable, but real. Check the Offers tab before placing any order; it costs nothing.

Option 3

Refer your own friends.

Every Wonder account has its own referral code, including yours. When someone uses your code as a new customer, they save $30 (the same way new users save with ANDY626), and you receive a referral credit toward your future Wonder orders.

The credit amount varies; in August 2026 it's typically $10 per successful referral, applied automatically to your next order. If you refer five friends who all order, that's $50 in Wonder credit, which is a real meal at Wonder's price points.

Your referral code lives in the Wonder app under Account, then "Refer & Earn" (or similar wording; Wonder reorganizes the menu occasionally). Share it on text, social media, or wherever else makes sense.

Verdict: the most reliable way to keep saving as an existing customer, but only if you actually have friends in Wonder's coverage area who haven't signed up yet.

The summary

What we'd actually recommend.

If you order Wonder regularly (more than twice a month), get Wonder+. The math is straightforward and you'll save consistently.

If you have a household member without a Wonder account, have them sign up with code ANDY626 for the new-customer $30. Then both of you place referrals back and forth as new friends try the service.

Check the app's Offers tab before each order. Restaurant promos and win-back deals appear there; they're usually small ($3-$10 off) but they're free money.

And don't trust generic "Wonder promo code for existing customers" results from coupon aggregators. Almost all of them are stale, and the ones that aren't are testing whether you'll click through and watch ads. The four options above are the real list.
