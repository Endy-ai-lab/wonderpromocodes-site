Wonder in Forest Hills

The Queens neighborhood with the densest co-op belt.

Forest Hills is the central spine of middle Queens, with one of the densest co-op apartment markets in the borough. The Tudor-and-prewar buildings along Yellowstone Boulevard, 71st Avenue, and Queens Boulevard hold tens of thousands of residents in a small area, most of them within walking distance of the 71st-Continental subway hub. Wonder's kitchen on Austin Street, on the main shopping strip, can reach almost all of them on the 35-minute promise.

The 35-minute zone covers Forest Hills proper, Rego Park to the north, Kew Gardens to the south, and parts of Briarwood and Forest Hills Gardens. The Forest Hills Gardens private streets are technically in zone, although couriers there have to navigate the gated layout, which adds a few minutes. Rego Park and the Lefrak City complex are dense enough to drive a steady share of orders.

Forest Hills runs on a different rhythm than Manhattan kitchens. Weekday dinner is the main demand window (6:30 to 9:00 pm), driven by commuters returning on the E, F, M, and R trains. Weekend brunch is meaningful but smaller than Manhattan. Late nights are quieter; the kitchen closes at 1:00 am because the after-hours demand drops off significantly past midnight. Forest Hills questions

Things people in Forest Hills ask before ordering.

Where is the Forest Hills Wonder kitchen?
71-14 Austin Street, on the main Forest Hills shopping strip. It's a few blocks from the 71st-Continental E/F/M/R hub. The kitchen has a small pickup counter; no significant dine-in.
Does Wonder deliver to Forest Hills Gardens?
Yes, the private-street section is in zone. Couriers have to navigate the gated layout, which can add a few minutes compared to the main grid. Most Forest Hills Gardens orders land in 25-30 minutes rather than the usual 22.
Does it cover Rego Park and the Lefrak City complex?
Yes. Rego Park north of Queens Boulevard and the entire Lefrak City complex are in zone. Lefrak deliveries hand off at the building lobby by default unless the order specifies otherwise.
Can it deliver to Briarwood and Kew Gardens?
Northern Briarwood near the Van Wyck Expressway is in zone, as is Kew Gardens north of Lefferts Boulevard. South of those, the addresses are at the boundary and sometimes route differently. The Wonder app picks the closer kitchen automatically.
How late is Forest Hills open?
1:00 am every night. Forest Hills late-night demand is smaller than Manhattan or Williamsburg, which is why this kitchen closes earlier than the urban late-night kitchens. The kitchen reopens at 10:30 am.
Does ANDY626 work in Forest Hills?
Yes. ANDY626 is a Wonder-wide referral code that works at every kitchen including Forest Hills. New customers save $15 on their first order and another $15 on their second order.
Is delivery faster from Wonder than DoorDash in Forest Hills?
Usually yes, because Wonder cooks the food at the same kitchen the courier leaves from. DoorDash routes a courier to a separate restaurant first, which adds 10-15 minutes on most Queens orders. Wonder also doesn't charge a delivery fee, while DoorDash typically adds $3-5.
