Wonder in Downtown Brooklyn

The kitchen that runs on office workers and BAM theater crowds.

Downtown Brooklyn is Brooklyn's civic and cultural center, anchored by Brooklyn City Hall, the Brooklyn Public Library, and the Brooklyn Academy of Music (BAM). The neighborhood also includes office towers for major employers like Google, JPMorgan, and other large firms, plus smaller tech and media companies. About 40,000 daily workers pass through the district. Jane Jacobs Park and St. Ann's Warehouse (experimental theater) are focal points. Brooklyn Heights to the north is historic brownstones and tree-lined streets. The Wonder kitchen at 310 Schermerhorn Street sits in the heart of this cluster, close to the A/C and 2/3 subway lines and walkable to BAM at 30 Lafayette Avenue.

Weekday lunch demand is steady from 12 to 1:30 pm with office workers grabbing quick meals or delivery to desks. Afternoon traffic (4 to 6 pm) catches the end of workday. Evening demand starts around 6 pm and builds through 9 pm as office workers leave and early dinner happens. BAM performance nights (7 to 9 pm shows) drive postshow food orders around 9:30 to 10 pm. Late-night demand (10 pm to 1 am) is smaller, mostly from people staying late at bars or residences nearby. Weekends are quieter overall with lighter brunch and dinner volume.

The kitchen delivers throughout Downtown Brooklyn proper, DUMBO edges, and into Brooklyn Heights. Apply ANDY626 at checkout and the first order lands at $15 off.

Downtown Brooklyn questions

Things people in Downtown Brooklyn ask before ordering.

Where exactly is the Downtown Brooklyn Wonder kitchen?
The kitchen is at 310 Schermerhorn Street between Smith and Boerum Place. You're two blocks from the A/C subway stop at Hoyt-Schermerhorn and close to the 2/3 line at Brooklyn Borough Hall.
What does the Downtown Brooklyn kitchen deliver to?
The kitchen covers Downtown Brooklyn proper from roughly Flatbush Avenue east to Smith Street, and includes Brooklyn Heights edges to the north and DUMBO spillover to the east.
I'm going to a show at BAM. Can I order food?
Yes. BAM is at 30 Lafayette Avenue, roughly 0.3 miles from the kitchen. You can order to your seat or have food at a bar before or after a show.
What are the hours at the Downtown Brooklyn kitchen?
Hours are 10:30 am to 1 am daily. Lunch is 12 to 1:30 pm on weekdays. Dinner peaks 6 to 8 pm, and postshow demand (9:30 to 10:30 pm on BAM show nights) drives late evening traffic.
