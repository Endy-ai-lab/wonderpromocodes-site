Wonder in Financial District

The kitchen that runs on Wall Street lunch rush and financial workers' tight schedules.

The Financial District is the oldest and most densely office-packed part of Manhattan. The NYSE and Federal Reserve are here. Office towers like 55 Water Street, the Morgan Stanley headquarters, and dozens of other skyscrapers hold about 50,000 daily workers. The neighborhood also includes South Street Seaport, Battery Park, and the 9/11 Memorial. The Wonder kitchen at 5 Hanover Street sits directly in the district, two blocks from the NYSE and Stone Street, the district's pedestrianized dining area. Subway access is via the 4/5 line and the Broadway/Nassau stop.

Weekday lunch demand is ferocious: 11:30 am to 1:30 pm brings nearly the entire financial services lunch crowd. Workers order from desks, trading floors, and street carts. Evening traffic (5 to 7 pm) catches the market close exodus and people heading to drinks on Stone Street or the Seaport. Late-night demand (10 pm to 1 am) comes from a younger crowd postgame at the nearby restaurants and bars, plus traders and back-office staff working late shifts. Weekends are very quiet.

The kitchen delivers throughout the Financial District's core and spillover into Lower Manhattan. Apply ANDY626 at checkout and the first order lands at $15 off.

Where exactly is the Financial District Wonder kitchen?
The kitchen is at 5 Hanover Street between Pearl and William Streets. You're two blocks from the NYSE, one block from Stone Street, and close to the 4/5 subway stop at Bowling Green.
What areas does the Financial District kitchen deliver to?
The kitchen covers the Financial District core from the Battery up to Chambers Street, with emphasis on Wall Street, Pearl Street, and the Seaport. It reaches most of Lower Manhattan south of City Hall.
Is lunch available during the market trading window?
Yes. Lunch is available throughout the trading day. Most orders come between 11:30 am and 1:30 pm when traders and office workers have their lunch break.

Hours are 10:30 am to 1 am daily. Lunch is the busiest period (11:30 am to 1:30 pm on weekdays). Evening orders run 5 to 7 pm, and late-night demand (10 pm to 1 am) comes from downtown bars and restaurants.
