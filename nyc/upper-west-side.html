Wonder in Upper West Side

From Lincoln Center to Columbia, on one menu.

The Upper West Side runs about fifty blocks north to south, from 59th Street up through 110th, with two of the densest residential corridors in Manhattan along Broadway and Amsterdam. Wonder picked the Broadway-and-75th area for the kitchen because it's the geographic middle of the demand: Lincoln Square to the south, Morningside Heights to the north, and the Central Park West blocks to the east all reach in under 25 minutes from there.

The UWS skews family in a way Williamsburg or the East Village do not, and that shapes what people order. Bellies (the kid-friendly partner) pulls more weight here than at most kitchens, and there's a real lunch-rush spike on weekdays from people working from home. Weekend brunch orders are the other big window: roughly 10:30 am to 1 pm on Saturdays and Sundays, the kitchen is the busiest it gets all week.

Columbia and Barnard are inside the zone, which makes this kitchen one of the better late options for the Morningside Heights crowd, even though it closes earlier than the downtown kitchens (midnight rather than 3 am). Orders to Columbia dorms typically take 25-30 minutes because the campus is at the far edge of the radius. ANDY626 still applies and stacks fine with the new-student traffic.

Upper West Side questions

Things people in Upper West Side ask before ordering.

Where is the Upper West Side Wonder?
2150 Broadway, between West 75th and West 76th Streets. It's a ground-floor kitchen with a small pickup counter, accessible from the 72nd Street 1/2/3 station and the 79th Street B/C station. There is no parking on Broadway, but UWS streets sometimes have meter spots an avenue over.
Does Wonder deliver to Columbia, Barnard, and Morningside Heights?
Yes. The entire Morningside Heights zone is covered, including Columbia, Barnard, Bank Street, Riverside Church, and the surrounding residential blocks. Orders to that area usually take 25-30 minutes because of the distance from the kitchen. Columbia dorm deliveries hand off at the front desk unless you specify otherwise.
Does it cover the Central Park West co-ops?
Yes, all of CPW is in zone. Couriers will hand off at the doorman's desk by default at any building that has one. If you want a buzz-up to the apartment, leave that note in the order. Orders to CPW addresses are usually faster than Broadway addresses (15-22 minutes) because they're closer to the kitchen.
How late does the UWS kitchen stay open?
Midnight every day. That's earlier than the Williamsburg or Hoboken kitchens (which run to 3-5 am) because UWS late-night demand is smaller. The kitchen opens at 10:30 am.
Why does my address near 60th Street sometimes route to Midtown West?
The two kitchens overlap at the 59th-60th Street boundary. The app picks whichever kitchen can deliver fastest at the moment you order, which depends on courier availability and traffic. Both kitchens carry the same menu and the ANDY626 discount applies identically.
Is brunch a busy time here?
Yes. Saturdays and Sundays from 10:30 am to about 1 pm are the busiest stretch the UWS kitchen sees all week. If you can order before 10:30 or after 1, expect orders 5-10 minutes faster. Weekday lunch (12-1 pm) is the second peak.
Can I use ANDY626 on a corporate or Wonder for Business order?
ANDY626 is for personal new-customer accounts. Wonder for Business has its own enterprise pricing and is handled separately. If you're a new individual user creating a personal account, the code works at the UWS kitchen the same as anywhere.
