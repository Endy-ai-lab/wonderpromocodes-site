Wonder in Midtown East

The kitchen that feeds Grand Central and Midtown's back-to-back lunch rush.

Midtown East is the core of Manhattan's business district, anchored by Grand Central Terminal on the south, the Chrysler Building at 42nd and Lexington, and office towers like Citicorp and Morgan Stanley north and east. The area has about 40,000 office workers on any given weekday, plus 750,000 daily Grand Central commuters. The Wonder kitchen at 800 3rd Avenue (near 52nd Street) sits in the heart of this density, within a five-minute walk of dozens of office buildings and the Lexington Avenue subway line. Nearby you'll find St. Bartholomew's Church, the Seagram Building, and the quiet Park Avenue co-op belt.

Lunch demand is relentless: 12 to 1:30 pm sees office workers, firm lunches, and delivery orders stacking up across nearly every weekday. Evening traffic (5:30 pm to 7 pm) catches commuters picking up food before heading to Grand Central or to bars on the East Side. Dinner orders slow after 8 pm as the neighborhood empties toward residential areas uptown and across bridges. Weekend volume is much lighter, mostly residents and tourists in transit.

The kitchen reaches most of Midtown East's core, from 40th to 59th Street between Park and the East River. Apply ANDY626 at checkout and the first order lands at $15 off.
