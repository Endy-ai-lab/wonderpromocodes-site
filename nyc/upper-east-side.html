Wonder in Upper East Side

From Lenox Hill to East Harlem, mostly older money and weekday lunches.

The Upper East Side runs roughly from 59th Street up through 96th, with East Harlem picking up north of that. Wonder placed the kitchen near 84th and Lexington because it's close to the geographic middle and a block off the 4/5/6 line, which couriers use to move quickly across the long avenue blocks. From there, the 35-minute zone reaches Lenox Hill in the south, Carnegie Hill and Yorkville in the middle, and the south edge of East Harlem at the top.

The order patterns here look different from most NYC neighborhoods. The UES skews older, more residential, and quieter at night. Weekday lunch (12 to 1:30 pm) is the busiest stretch most weeks because of the high concentration of work-from-home professionals along Park, Madison, and Fifth. Weekend brunch is the second peak, although smaller than UWS. Late nights are slow; the kitchen closes at midnight and rarely sees a rush after 10 pm.

Two practical things to know about UES delivery. First, almost every building above 70th has a doorman, and couriers will hand off at the desk by default unless you specifically request a buzz-up. Second, the East River high-rise corridor between York and FDR Drive is technically inside the zone but takes longer (28-32 minutes) because of the avenue distance. ANDY626 still applies and the discount is the same.

Upper East Side questions

Things people in Upper East Side ask before ordering.

Where is the Upper East Side Wonder kitchen?
120 East 86th Street, between East 84th and East 85th Streets. It's a ground-floor kitchen with a small pickup counter, accessible from the 86th Street 4/5/6 station. Parking on Lexington is metered with limited turnover; couriers use bikes or scooters by default.
Does Wonder deliver to East Harlem?
Yes, the southern part of East Harlem (96th to roughly 110th) is in the UES zone. Above 110th Street, the app routes to the Central Harlem kitchen instead. The boundary is automatic; you don't need to pick a kitchen yourself.
Does it cover the Park, Madison, and Fifth Avenue co-ops?
Yes. The full prewar belt along Park, Madison, and Fifth is in zone. Couriers default to handing off at the doorman's desk. If you want the bag brought up, leave a note in the order.
How late does the UES kitchen stay open?
Midnight every day. The UES has lower late-night demand than downtown neighborhoods, which is why the kitchen closes earlier than Williamsburg or East Village. The kitchen reopens at 10:30 am.
Why does my address near 96th sometimes route to Central Harlem?
The two kitchens overlap at 96th-110th. The Wonder app picks whichever can deliver fastest at the moment you order, which depends on courier availability and time of day. Both kitchens have the same chef partners and the ANDY626 discount applies identically.
Does Wonder deliver to Roosevelt Island?
Roosevelt Island's southern half is technically in the UES zone, but actual delivery is harder because of the bridge access. Orders sometimes take 35-40 minutes there. North Roosevelt Island is generally outside the zone.
Is ANDY626 still valid for UES orders?
Yes. ANDY626 is a single Wonder-wide referral code that works at every kitchen across the Northeast. New customers ordering from the UES kitchen get $15 off their first order and another $15 off their second order, $30 total.
