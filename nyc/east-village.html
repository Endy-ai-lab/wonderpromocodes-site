Wonder in East Village

The kitchen that actually stays open until 5 am.

The East Village is the densest bar district in Manhattan and one of the few places where late-night food demand is loud enough to support a 5 am closing. Wonder's First Avenue kitchen runs to 5 am on Friday and Saturday nights, 3 am the rest of the week. That makes it one of the most useful late-night options south of 14th Street, especially after kitchens like Veselka and Mamoun's have hit their limits.

The 35-minute delivery zone covers the East Village proper from Houston up to 14th, all of Alphabet City through Avenue D, the Lower East Side down to roughly Delancey, and a slice of NoHo east of Broadway. South of Delancey usually routes to the FiDi or Lower Manhattan kitchen instead because of the bridge traffic patterns.

The order rhythm is unusual here. Weekday lunch is light. Weekday evenings between 7 and 10 pm are normal. The actual peak is the post-bar rush, midnight through 2 am on weekends, when couriers are running back-to-back orders. If you order at 1:30 am on a Friday, allow a few extra minutes; if you order at 6 pm Tuesday, expect 18-22 minutes flat. ANDY626 applies regardless of when you order.

East Village questions

Things people in East Village ask before ordering.

Where is the East Village Wonder kitchen?
10 Stuyvesant St, between East 9th Street and 3rd Ave. It's a ground-floor kitchen with a small pickup counter and a few seats for waiting. The Astor Place 6 station and the First Avenue L stop are both close by.
How late does it really stay open?
5:00 am Friday and Saturday. 3:00 am Sunday through Thursday. The kitchen reopens at 10:30 am every day. These are real hours, not pickup-only late hours; full delivery service runs the entire window.
Does Wonder deliver all the way to Avenue D?
Yes. All of Alphabet City through Avenue D is in zone. Avenue D addresses sometimes take 25-30 minutes because they're at the eastern edge, but the kitchen reaches them. The deeper East Village blocks between Avenues A and C are the densest delivery area.
Where does the LES boundary sit?
The Lower East Side splits at roughly Delancey Street. North of Delancey routes to the East Village kitchen; south of Delancey usually routes to the FiDi kitchen. The Wonder app picks the closer kitchen automatically based on your address.
Is the East Village kitchen busy on weekend nights?
Yes, especially midnight to 2 am Fridays and Saturdays. That's the post-bar rush, and orders during that window can take 28-32 minutes instead of the usual 20. If you can order before midnight, expect faster delivery. The 5 am closing time is intentional; the kitchen sees real volume right up to closing.
Does ANDY626 work for late-night East Village orders?
Yes. ANDY626 has no time-of-day restrictions. New customers ordering at 4 am get the same $15 off as new customers ordering at 4 pm.
Can I order from East Village to a friend in Brooklyn?
No. The Wonder app routes orders to the closest kitchen based on the delivery address. A Brooklyn delivery address will use a Brooklyn kitchen, not the East Village kitchen. The chef partners and prices are nearly identical, so the actual order looks the same regardless.
