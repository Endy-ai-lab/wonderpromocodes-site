Wonder in Williamsburg

Late-night Brooklyn's most useful kitchen.

Williamsburg is the northern half of the brownstone-Brooklyn nightlife belt, with a population that skews young, earns well, and orders food at hours most kitchens have stopped cooking. Wonder leaned into that. The Williamsburg kitchen runs until 5:00 am on Friday and Saturday nights and 3:00 am the rest of the week, which makes it one of the most useful late-night options in north Brooklyn after the bars close.

The 35-minute zone covers everything from McCarren Park down to Broadway, from the East River back to Bushwick Avenue. North Williamsburg (Bedford to McCarren) and the South Williamsburg waterfront are the densest delivery areas. The kitchen also reaches a chunk of Greenpoint to the north and parts of West Bushwick toward the L train. Long Island City addresses on the Brooklyn-Queens Expressway side often route here too because of the creek.

Two practical tips for Williamsburg orders. The kitchen is busiest between 7 pm and 9 pm and again from midnight to 2 am, so if you can shift around those windows, expect 5-10 fewer minutes. And couriers here use bikes more than cars because of how the streets work; if you're in a walk-up, expect them to call rather than buzz. ANDY626 still gets you the $30 off either way.

Williamsburg questions

Things people in Williamsburg ask before ordering.

Where is the Williamsburg Wonder location?
195 Wythe Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11211. It's in North Williamsburg, between North 5th and North 6th, a couple of blocks from the Bedford L stop and the same distance from the East River ferry pier. Pickup is available at the front counter; the dine-in space is small but exists.
How late does it stay open?
5:00 am Friday and Saturday nights, 3:00 am Sunday through Thursday. That makes it one of the latest food options in Williamsburg by a comfortable margin. Most full-service spots are closed by 1 am even on weekends.
Does Wonder deliver to Greenpoint and Bushwick?
Greenpoint south of Calyer Street is fully in zone. North Greenpoint (toward Newtown Creek and the Pulaski) is at the edge and sometimes routes to LIC instead. West Bushwick (the Morgan and Jefferson L stops) is in zone; East Bushwick beyond Broadway has its own dedicated kitchen.
Does Wonder Williamsburg do bike delivery?
Most Williamsburg orders go out by e-bike rather than by car, which is faster than a car here given the one-way streets and lack of parking. Couriers will usually call instead of buzzing if you're in a walk-up. The bike system doesn't change pricing or the ANDY626 discount.
Can I order from Williamsburg to Manhattan?
No. The Wonder app routes your order to the closest kitchen automatically based on your delivery address. A Manhattan address won't route to the Williamsburg kitchen even if you can see it from your window. If you want food delivered to a Manhattan address, the app will use the East Village or Lower East Side kitchen instead.
Is it cheaper to order Wonder than DoorDash in Williamsburg?
On a per-order basis, almost always yes. Wonder doesn't charge a delivery fee, while DoorDash typically adds $3-5 plus surge pricing. Wonder also doesn't mark up menu prices the way DoorDash often does. With ANDY626 applied, your first two orders are $15 off each, which puts you well below DoorDash on roughly any comparable cart.
What's the dine-in space like?
Small. Maybe a dozen seats at a counter and a few high-tops. It's not really set up for a proper sit-down meal; most people use it for waiting on a pickup order or grabbing a quick lunch. The full menu is available there and ANDY626 applies to dine-in orders the same way as delivery.
