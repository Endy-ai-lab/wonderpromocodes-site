Wonder in Lenox Hill

The Lex 6 corridor between Bloomingdale's and the Met.

Lenox Hill runs roughly from 60th Street to 77th Street, between Fifth Avenue and the East River, anchored by the 6 train along Lexington and the long block of Park Avenue co-ops. Wonder's kitchen at 888 Lexington (between 66th and 67th) sits in the practical middle: a few minutes' walk from Hunter College, Memorial Sloan Kettering, and the Bloomingdale's corner.

The order pattern here is unusual because Lenox Hill has two demand layers. The residential layer is older money, weekend brunch, and weekday dinner, mostly to the long apartment blocks along Park, Madison, and Lex. The professional layer is Memorial Sloan Kettering and New York-Presbyterian Hospital, both in the zone, both ordering steadily through the day for staff and family of patients. Hospital deliveries to the main MSK and NYP entrances are routine; the front desks know how to hand off Wonder orders.

The 35-minute zone covers all of Lenox Hill proper, Sutton Place to the east of Third Avenue, and a slice of northern Midtown East down to about 57th Street. The southern UES below 77th is fully in zone too, although the Upper East Side kitchen at 86th picks up the demand above that. The two kitchens cover the same neighborhood but at different latitudes, with the Wonder app routing automatically based on the address.

Lenox Hill questions

Things people in Lenox Hill ask before ordering.

Where is the Lenox Hill Wonder kitchen?
888 Lexington Avenue, between East 66th and East 67th Streets. It's a ground-floor kitchen accessible from the 68th Street-Hunter College 6 station. Pickup is available at a small counter.
Does Wonder deliver to Memorial Sloan Kettering and NYP?
Yes, both are in zone. The kitchen sees regular hospital deliveries from staff, visiting family, and patients on outpatient schedules. Front desks at the main MSK entrance on East 68th and the main NYP entrance on East 68th near York are familiar with handing off Wonder orders. Leave a note with the recipient's name and unit if relevant.
What's the difference between the Lenox Hill and Upper East Side kitchens?
Geography. Lenox Hill covers roughly 57th to 77th Street; the UES kitchen at 86th covers 77th and above through East Harlem. Same chef partners, same menu, same ANDY626 discount. The Wonder app picks the closer kitchen based on your delivery address.
Does it cover Sutton Place and Beekman Place?
Yes. Both Sutton Place east of Third Avenue and the Beekman Place blocks down toward 49th are in zone. Doorman buildings get lobby handoff by default; specify if you want bag delivery to the apartment.
How late does Lenox Hill stay open?
Midnight every night. The kitchen reopens at 10:30 am. Lenox Hill late-night demand is smaller than downtown neighborhoods, which is why this kitchen closes earlier than the East Village or Hudson Square kitchens.
Why does my address near 77th sometimes route to the UES kitchen?
77th is the rough boundary between the two kitchens. The Wonder app picks whichever is faster at the moment you order, which depends on courier availability. Both kitchens have identical menus and accept ANDY626.
Does ANDY626 work at the Lenox Hill kitchen?
