Wonder Delivery Zones: How to Check If You're Covered (And What to Do If You're Not)

Published June 8, 2026 · Aaron

One of the most common questions we get is some version of “does Wonder deliver to my address?” The answer depends on where you live relative to one of Wonder’s ~120 kitchens. Each kitchen has a delivery radius of roughly 35 minutes by scooter or car, which translates to about 2-4 miles depending on traffic and geography.

Here’s how to check whether you’re covered, how the zones work, and what your options are if you’re outside one.

How to check if Wonder delivers to you

The fastest way is to open the Wonder app or go to wonder.com, enter your address, and see if any kitchens show up. If you’re in the coverage area, the app will show you the nearest kitchen and the available restaurants.

If you want to see the full list of locations before downloading the app, we maintain a complete directory of every Wonder kitchen organized by state. Each location page shows the specific neighborhoods that kitchen delivers to, the address, and the hours.

Which states does Wonder cover?

As of mid-2026, Wonder operates in 11 states across the Northeast:

  • New York City — ~30+ kitchens across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and Staten Island
  • New Jersey — 20+ kitchens from Hoboken to Cherry Hill
  • New York (Long Island & Westchester) — 15+ kitchens from Bellmore to Scarsdale
  • Pennsylvania — 15+ kitchens in Philadelphia and suburbs
  • Massachusetts — 10+ kitchens in Greater Boston
  • Connecticut — 3 kitchens in Fairfield County
  • Maryland — 5 kitchens in Baltimore and Annapolis areas
  • Washington DC — 5 kitchens across DC neighborhoods
  • Virginia — 6 kitchens in Northern Virginia
  • Delaware — 2 kitchens in Newark and Wilmington
  • Rhode Island — 2 kitchens in Cranston and East Providence

How the delivery radius works

Each Wonder kitchen delivers within roughly a 35-minute radius. In dense urban areas like Manhattan or Hoboken, that radius might be 1-2 miles because traffic is slow. In suburban areas like Long Island or New Jersey, it might be 3-4 miles because the roads are faster.

The radius isn’t a perfect circle — it’s shaped by geography (rivers, highways, parks) and road access. A kitchen in Hoboken, for example, covers all of Hoboken and parts of Weehawken and Jersey City Heights, but not all of Jersey City (that’s a separate kitchen). A kitchen in Williamsburg covers Williamsburg and parts of Greenpoint and Bushwick, but not all of Brooklyn.

The best way to know if your specific address is covered is to check the Wonder app. But as a general rule: if you’re within 2-3 miles of a Wonder kitchen and there are no major geographic barriers (a river with no bridge, a highway with no crossing), you’re probably in the zone.

What happens if you’re outside the zone

If your address is outside the delivery radius of any Wonder kitchen, the app will tell you “delivery not available in your area.” You have a few options:

1. Check if a nearby kitchen covers you. Wonder is expanding rapidly — new kitchens open every few months. Check the locations directory periodically to see if a new kitchen has opened closer to you.

2. Try pickup instead of delivery. If you’re outside the delivery zone but can get to a Wonder kitchen, pickup is usually ready in 12-15 minutes. You can still use the ANDY626 referral code on pickup orders — the $30 discount applies regardless of fulfillment method.

3. Use a different address. If you work or spend time inside a coverage zone, you can have Wonder delivered there. The app lets you save multiple addresses.

4. Wait. Wonder is opening new kitchens aggressively — they went from 28 kitchens in early 2025 to about 120 in mid-2026. If you’re in a Northeast metro area that doesn’t have coverage yet, there’s a reasonable chance one is coming.
