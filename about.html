About

About Wonder Promo Code.

Wonder Promo Code is an independent referral site for Wonder food delivery. The site exists to share the referral code ANDY626 with new Wonder customers, who get $15 off their first order and another $15 off their second order ($30 total) when they apply it.

There is no other relationship with Wonder. The site is not operated by, endorsed by, or paid for by Wonder Group, Inc.

Who runs this site.

My name is Aaron. I'm an independent affiliate operator based in the Northeast. I started this site after spending too many hours comparing Wonder, DoorDash, and UberEats for my own orders and realizing the referral code system was genuinely confusing for new customers. Most coupon sites either list stale codes that don't work or bury the actual code behind invasive ads. I wanted something cleaner.

How we verify the code.

On the first of every month, I manually test the referral link by opening it in a clean browser session and confirming that ANDY626 auto-fills at Wonder's checkout and that the $15-off-first-order discount appears. I also check that the second-order $15 discount is still active by reviewing the terms shown at checkout. If either discount has changed, the site is updated within 24 hours.

The "Last verified" date shown across the site reflects the most recent build, which corresponds to the most recent monthly check. The code has been continuously active since August 2025.

How we handle location data.

Each Wonder location page includes the kitchen's address, hours, delivery radius, and the neighborhoods it reaches. This data is sourced from Wonder's public website and app, and is cross-checked against Google Maps for address accuracy. When a kitchen changes its hours, moves, or closes, the site is updated on the next monthly rebuild. If you spot an error, email the contact address on the contact page and it will get fixed.

How this site makes money.

The only revenue is a small referral credit that Wonder pays when a new customer places their first order using ANDY626. There are no ads, no tracking pixels, no retargeting cookies, and no third-party advertising scripts. The full disclosure is on the disclosure page.

Editorial independence.

The reviews, comparisons, and guides on this site reflect honest opinions. When Wonder is worse than a competitor in a specific scenario, the site says so. The Wonder vs DoorDash comparison explicitly lists cases where DoorDash is the better choice. No content on this site has been reviewed or approved by Wonder, and no content has been changed at Wonder's request.

Update history.

The site is rebuilt on the first of every month with refreshed location data, re-tested promo codes, and any new Wonder kitchen openings. Major content updates (new guides, new comparison pages) are announced on the blog.
