Wonder in College Park

The kitchen that feeds UMD students studying late and living on campus.

College Park is the home of the University of Maryland, with the main campus occupying about 1,300 acres in the center of town. The university has approximately 47,000 students (including about 27,000 undergraduates) and is Maryland's flagship public research university. Baltimore Avenue is the main commercial spine along the east side of campus, lined with restaurants, bars, student services, and bookstores. The surrounding town has about 32,000 permanent residents plus the student body, creating a young, education-focused demographic. The Metro system (Green Line) connects College Park directly to Washington DC about 10 miles south. University housing and fraternity/sorority life dominate much of the surrounding residential area. The Wonder kitchen at 7423 Baltimore Avenue sits directly on the main campus-facing commercial strip, within steps of dormitories and academic buildings.

Weekday lunch demand is moderate from 12 to 1:30 pm from students between classes and local office workers. Afternoon traffic (3 to 6 pm) is heavy as students come out of afternoon classes and head to dorms or libraries. Dinner peaks from 6 to 8 pm as students eat before studying or socializing. Late-night demand (10 pm to 1:30 am) is the second-busiest window, driven by students studying late for exams or working on assignments, plus social gatherings and bars on Baltimore Avenue. Weekends see lighter lunch but sustained evening traffic and strong late-night demand as students socialize and study.
