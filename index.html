Frequently asked

Questions before you order.

Is ANDY626 a real promo code?
Yes. ANDY626 is a referral code on Wonder's official referral program. New Wonder customers get $15 off their first order and another $15 off their second order, for $30 total. We re-test the code on the first of every month and the page is dated accordingly. Most recently verified August 1, 2026.
How much will I actually save?
$30 total. That's $15 off your first order and $15 off your second. The discount applies to your subtotal before tax. Wonder doesn't charge a delivery fee on top, so the code's full value goes to your food.
Does the code work for existing Wonder customers?
No, Wonder's referral program is new-customer only. If you already have a Wonder account, ANDY626 won't apply. We track every active option for existing customers here (Wonder+ membership savings, restaurant-specific promos, etc).
Does Wonder deliver to my address?
Wonder operates across NY, NJ, MA, MD, CT, PA, and a handful of other Northeast markets. Each location delivers within a 35-minute radius. Check the locations page to see if you're in the zone.
Is delivery actually free?
Yes. Wonder doesn't charge a delivery fee on any order, no minimum, no membership required. There's a small service fee on most orders ($1.99-$2.99) which Wonder+ members ($7.99/month) can remove.
What is Wonder+ and is it worth it?
Wonder+ is a $7.99/month membership that removes the service fee and adds occasional member-only discounts. If you order more than twice a month, it usually pays for itself. New customers should still use ANDY626 first to lock in the $30. Wonder+ doesn't block the new-user code.
